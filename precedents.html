Summer internship registration and faculty compensation at other universities

Policies and models from other institutions that Suffolk could learn from. Each link opens the institution’s own page.

Summer internships with fall registration or academic credit

  • Stonehill College: Summer Internship Policy
    Students complete internship hours during summer, with academic work continuing into fall. Students register in both terms, but tuition is included in fall billing.
  • Tulane University (Freeman School of Business): For-Credit Internship Program
    Summer internships are registered for fall credit to avoid summer tuition. An exception allows summer enrollment with a fee waiver when employers require concurrent registration.
  • Tulane University (Center for Public Service): Registration and Grading
    Separates summer internship participation from fall academic credit and grading, including zero-credit experiential recognition.
  • University of Richmond: English Department Internship Policy
    Permits summer internships to receive academic credit in fall. Requires advance spring approval, with academic assignments and grading determined by faculty.
  • UC Davis: Internship Frequently Asked Questions
    Students may complete internship hours and assignments during summer, enroll in fall, and receive academic credit at the end of fall.
  • University of Minnesota: Internship Academic Credit Policy | Implementation FAQ
    Allows credit during the internship or in the immediately subsequent term. Requires advance agreements, academic coursework, an instructor, and university contact during the experience.

Faculty compensation and workload models

  • Elon University: Faculty Compensation Policy
    Addresses internship supervision through regular teaching load, overload compensation, and banked workload credits toward future course releases.
  • Indiana University Kokomo: Internship Faculty Salary Policy
    Encourages bundling internship supervision into regular teaching assignments, with alternative tuition-linked compensation when that is not possible.
  • California State University, San Bernardino: Faculty Assigned-Time Guidelines (PDF)
    Establishes workload calculations for individual internship supervision versus scheduled internship courses with larger enrollments.

Other useful approaches and comparison institutions

  • Brandeis University: HSSP Internship Program
    Requires advance approval and at least 110 internship hours. Students may complete the associated academic seminar concurrently or afterward.
  • Johns Hopkins University: Research, Internships, and Independent Study
    Offers approved summer internship and research credits without summer tuition charges.
  • Coastal Carolina University: Internship Options
    Distinguishes paid credit-bearing registration, tuition-free zero-credit transcript recognition, and noncredit internship documentation.
  • Hamilton College: Credit for Internships
    Uses departmental approval, employer evaluation, and post-internship academic reflection.
  • Clark University: Academic Internship Program
    Massachusetts private-university comparison with structured faculty sponsorship and academic approval.
  • Providence College: Summer Tuition Policy
    Offers reduced summer internship tuition rather than deferring credit to fall.
  • American University: School of Communication Internship Credit
    Internship credit falls within regular full-time tuition during fall/spring but incurs per-credit tuition during summer.

Most relevant to Suffolk: Stonehill, Richmond, Tulane, UC Davis, and Minnesota provide particularly useful precedents for spring approval, summer experiences, and fall academic credit. Elon and Indiana University Kokomo offer particularly relevant faculty workload and compensation models.
